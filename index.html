Our Story

Founded in 2010, SJ Construction has grown from a small team of passionate architects into a full‑service design‑build firm. Our mission is to create environments that reflect the unique vision of each client while adhering to the highest standards of sustainability and craftsmanship.

We collaborate closely with interior designers, engineers, and contractors to ensure every project—whether residential, commercial, or hospitality—delivers on time, on budget, and beyond expectations.
